About Hans Lazaro Wilson

Visual Storyteller, Designer & Endurance Athlete

Hey! I’m Hans. Based in Bahrain, I operate at the intersection of graphic design, high-contrast photography, and high-energy athletics. My creative work is driven by dark aesthetic tones, dramatic light play, and modern editorial styling.

Whether I am crafting main poster designs for brand campaigns, shooting high-horsepower automotors on night tracks, capturing sweeping land landscapes, or documenting long-distance runs on Strava, I bring raw focus and premium execution.
